`nymbal import --dry-run` lists a record it cannot read under Errors, counted as refused, instead of stopping, and refuses a product whose own variations share a SKU, as the import does.
`--concurrency` (and the `concurrency` option) must be a whole number of 1 or more; `--entities` help names all five entities and the real default.
