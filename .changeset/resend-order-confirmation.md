The admin's Resend confirmation now sends the order email again, with a link that opens the order through a new key; a refused send is retried. The new event is `order.confirmation_resend_requested.v1`.
`POST /api/admin/orders/:id/resend-confirmation` now answers `{ orderId, orderNumber, email }`; `sent` is gone. Pending and cancelled orders answer 409 `order.unpaid`.
