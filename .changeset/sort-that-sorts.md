Sort works. `GET /api/products?sort=` takes `newest` (the default), `price-asc`, `price-desc`, `name-asc` or `name-desc`, ties broken by slug; an unknown key, or a cursor from another sort, answers 400. Breaking in the SDK: `productList.setSort(key)` takes a `ProductSortKey` instead of `(field, direction)`, `state.sort` is that key. `load(params)` defines the listing: its `sort` and `category` become the active ones, and a `load(params)` without `sort` resets the sort to `newest`; `load()` with no arguments reloads the current sort and category, and `setSort` and `loadMore` keep them. A response that arrives after a newer request is dropped. `QueryParams.orderBy` orders a document-store query without filtering it. Both templates offer the same five sorts, keep them in `?sort=`, and render that order on the server. Category facet counts on `GET /api/products` now always count the whole catalogue.
